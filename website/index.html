PIGEON / Workspace
One considered workspace

Room for the whole workflow.

Move from a question to a research report, a draft, or a finished task. Keep the tools you need within reach.

01 / CONVERSE

Chat & agents

Streaming answers, local and cloud models, tool approvals, MCP connections, memory, skills, and attachments.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Research & knowledge

Deep research, source-backed reports, personal documents, retrieval, and a library that stays close to your work.

03 / CREATE

Writing & images

A document editor with AI assistance and versions. A gallery and image editor with layers, masks, and export tools.

04 / ORGANIZE

Your daily tools

Email and contacts, notes, calendars, reminders, and scheduled tasks, together in the workspace.

05 / EXPLORE

Model workflows

Compare models side by side, vote blind, and use hardware-aware recommendations for local downloads and serving.

06 / PERSONALIZE

Make it familiar

A calm Midnight theme, custom colors, alternative fonts, density controls, keyboard shortcuts, and mobile layouts.

Designed for sustained work

Less visual noise.
More room to think.

Charcoal surfaces, warm typography, and deliberate accents. PIGEON's identity is vivid; its working space stays calm.

Control stays with you.

  • Run the application on infrastructure you choose.
  • Connect local models or your preferred cloud providers.
  • Keep authentication and tool approvals in your workflow.
  • Inspect and adapt the source under its open-source license.
